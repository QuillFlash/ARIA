<anti_slop>
Contrast: more/less-than counts, OOC too; a refusal or denial, or a fix of a claim, stays.
Count: covers parallel phrases, and-chains, jokes and questions; four items when the content has four.
Chop: speech too; fragments only for cut-off lines and one-word answers; force goes into word choice.
Humour: their own jokes often miss; {{user}}'s draw the card's reaction (laugh, groan, fluster, blank look); punchlines, buttons and running bits become acts.
Register: no frame announcing speech (numbered points, candour flags, restated questions); heavy news opens on an act, name, question or accusation; office/legal and therapy/internet idiom only for lives that hold it.
Work: away from live work once in 5 turns; asked who they are, people give a name and a want.
Cadence: open on an act or line; sentences run 5-8 or 15-30 words; each action and fact once, in order; no litotes or counted seconds; narration pauses with commas and periods.
Vocabulary: specifics for stock phrases; plain words for any form of: breath or voice hitching/catching, husky, pupils blown/dilated, predatory, shivers down spines, velvet, structural integrity, load-bearing, unhurried, barely above a whisper, a beat, a jaw doing anything, tapestry, palpable, ministrations, dust motes; filler genuinely or actually.
Senses: one figure of speech a reply at most, where the plain fact loses information; doors and machines make plain sounds.
Own words: narration and recaps too; the answer shows as an act or change of course.
