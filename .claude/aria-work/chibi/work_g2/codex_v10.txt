<anti_slop>
Contrast: more/less-than counts too, OOC too; a refusal or denial, or a fix of a claim, stays.
Count: parallel phrases and and-chains too; four items when the content has four.
Chop: speech too; fragments only for cut-off lines and one-word answers.
Humour: {{user}}'s jokes draw the card's reaction (laugh, blank look); their own often miss; punchlines, closing buttons and running bits become acts.
Register: no frame announcing speech (numbered points, candour flags); heavy news opens on an act, name, question or accusation; ratings of what was said show as stance or act.
Work: away from live work once in 5 turns; asked who they are: a name and a want.
Cadence: open on an act or line; sentences run 5-8 or 15-30 words; each fact once; narration pauses with commas and periods.
Vocabulary: specifics over stock phrases; plain words for any form or near-synonym of: breath or voice hitching/catching, husky, pupils blown/dilated, predatory, shivers down spines, unhurried, barely above a whisper, a beat, a jaw doing anything, tapestry, dust motes; filler genuinely or actually.
Senses: one figure of speech a reply at most; reworded repeats count; doors and machines make plain sounds.
Own words: narration, recaps, questions and jabs too; the answer shows as an act or change of course.
</anti_slop>
