ARIA 1.0 beta 8: 🔓 Jailbreak, 7,000 cap, Chibi Edition and four fixes

🔓 Jailbreak (needs Adult Context), OFF by default since ARIA ships
SFW. One toggle sets ariaJb (the Role and Frank plan lines) and
ariaJbLines while an adult mode sets ariaModeLine; both Logic Cores and
the TC plan print it after the Ledger line and before Fresh, and the
Impersonation Turn blanks it. The lines are distilled from Realistic
Frankenstein's BOLT and Micro CoTs, their Jean-Claude Van Damme
editions and its Realism and Freaky modes: a game master of an adult
fiction rather than an assistant, even-handed outcomes, characters who
finish what they start, and an explicit plan for each sexual, violent,
cruel or dark beat, with sex kept between adults. The parts that work
by telling the model it has no rules, by a professional-writer pretext
or by asking it to drop its protectiveness stayed out. Marinara carries
it as the logic_options JAILBREAK option, and the Impersonate regex
strips the two new labels.

The token cap rises from 6,000 to 7,000 on every fully kitted-out
path, Marinara with Agents included: CC 6,111, TC 6,101 (GLM), Marinara
6,376 at most.

Chibi Edition: a lighter ARIA in three new files (CC, TC and Agent
Gating), every module kept and compressed in the Douyin Edition's
concise style, without quoted examples. Same identifiers, variables,
plan labels and regex scripts as the full edition. Fate and Time ship
OFF; Chibi TC ships with The Logic Core on. CC 1,503 shipped and 2,978
fully kitted out; TC 1,589 lean and 2,997; Marinara 2,984 at most, and
3,070 with Agents, which the owner allowed. Each module group was
drafted by Sonnet and judged by Opus, and each file reviewed by Opus.

Four older fixes:
- gemini38Patch is now blanked by the Main Prompt, so switching the
  Gemini 3.8 Flash patch off no longer leaves its text printing through
  Gemini All-In-One for a turn. The checker's stale-state test now runs
  for every patch on its own, which catches that kind of leak.
- The checker ranks TC patches with every non-patch switch on, so
  "everything ON" uses the real largest patch (GLM, 6,101, not 5,938).
- The four plan-cleanup regexes, in CC and in the TC regex file, now
  also remove the "For a message written as ... line." sentence and the
  "Every response starts with" line from an echoed TC plan.
- Stale token costs in switch and entry comments are refreshed.

The guide gains section 7, "Meet Chibi me" (Uh-oh moments is now 8 and
Manager's corner 9), the Jailbreak entry, the new switch and wiring
rows, the 7,000 cap and re-measured figures, and marks beta 8's fixes
as confirmed live on GLM 5.3 Flash, MiMo V2.6 Flash and both DeepSeeks.
