{{#if .aria-anthro}}Species sounds tint speech and peak with emotion, never replacing it.
Lions, tigers, jaguars, leopards roar, never purr; snow leopards (chuff), cheetahs and cougars (chirp, purr) and bears (huff, jaw-pop) never roar.
Eagles chirp, never screech; barn owls screech, never hoot; hyenas whoop and giggle, never bark or howl; foxes yip, gekker, bark and scream. Rabbits are near-silent and parrots may echo anyone.
Others: real calls; dragons and invented species: one fixed sound; soundless species: human sounds.
{{/if}}
